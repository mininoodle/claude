# IMAGERY.md — real flower imagery replaces the drawn flowers (binding contract)

The user asked: "replace the flowers you generated with both these and similar ones you can generate, use image generation over css drawings. apply the same monochrome filter." So every flower the app shows becomes a real raster image: photographs, paintings and ink drawings, either supplied by the user or generated by an AI image model in the same styles. The canvas-drawn silhouettes of `src/flora.js` and the canvas-drawn covers of `src/data.js` go away. The runtime media filter (`--fx`, DESIGN.md section 4) stays exactly as it is and is what makes the images monochrome by default; the image files themselves keep whatever colour they have, so the `color` and `cyano` modes still have something to show.

Everything else in DESIGN.md and SPEC.md still holds. Where this document and DESIGN.md disagree, this document wins.

## 1. Sources

`imagery/source/` holds the six images the user supplied (read-only). `imagery/generated/` holds the generated ones, named by key. `imagery/probe/` holds two early generations (`lily.jpg`, `branch.jpg`) that are also usable sources.

| file | what it is | use |
|---|---|---|
| `source/2.jpg` | painting: a blossoming branch over a dark teal pond, petals floating in rings, white background at the top (portrait 736×1308) | cover crops from the pond area and the branch over water; never show the white top edge in a cover |
| `source/3.jpg` | monochrome photograph: a white water lily on black water with duckweed (portrait) | cover crops; also the duckweed texture |
| `source/4.jpg` | photograph: cherry blossoms floating on blue rippled water. **Carries a photographer's watermark** ("yusa__style_") right of centre, about 55–85% across and 52–58% down | cover crops that exclude the watermark region entirely |
| `source/5.jpg` | colour photograph: pink water lilies and pads (landscape 1200×900) | cover crops (the colour modes rely on a few colour images) |
| `source/6.jpg` | white embroidered floral garland on black (portrait) | decoration source (sprigs, garland) and cover crops |
| `source/7.webp` | tattoo flash on a flat mid-grey ground: a cherry blossom branch (top-left two thirds), a lily with ornament (top-right), a winged cherub figure (bottom third) | decoration source: key the grey to transparent; use ONLY the blossom branch and the lily; never the cherub |
| `probe/lily.jpg` | generated monochrome water lily with a thin white frame around the image | cover crops inset past the frame |
| `probe/branch.jpg` | generated white ink cherry branch on pure black | decoration (branch, sprigs) and cover |
| `generated/cov_*.jpg` | generated cover sources, see the key list in `imagery/urls.json` | covers |

Key prefixes: `cov_<species>_bw` monochrome photographs; `cov_<species>_ink` white ink on black; `cov_pond_paint*`, `cov_petals_water*` painted or photographed petals on dark water; `cov_garland*`, `cov_lace` embroidered white on black; `cov_<species>_colour` muted colour photographs; `deco_*` decorations; `petals_sheet*` petal sheets.

Every file is data, not code. Nothing in `imagery/` is served or shipped as is: the pipeline below produces the shipped files.

Quality notes from a review of the contact sheets (`dist/sheet-covers.html`, `dist/sheet-deco.html`, screenshots in `tools/shots/orchestrator/`):
- Thin white frames around the image: `cov_daisy_ink`, `cov_hibiscus_bw`, `cov_lily_bw2`, `cov_moonflower_ink`, `cov_wisteria_ink` (left edge), `deco_bud`, `deco_sprig_peony`, `deco_sprig_wisteria`, `probe/lily.jpg`. Detect frames generically (scan inward from each edge while the row or column is near white or near black and uniform) and inset every crop past them.
- `deco_branch_home2` is black ink on a WHITE ground: invert it first, then key like the others.
- `deco_garland_vertical` sits on a very dark green ground, not pure black: measure the background level from the corners of every decoration source and key relative to it (alpha = clamp((luma − bg − 10) / 50)), so no haze rectangle survives under the invert filter.
- `cov_petals_water2` is very dark; use it sparingly and lift its mid-tones a little when cropping.
- `cov_garland2` is a square wreath and `cov_lace` a repeating pattern: good for wallpaper-like covers and the `mosaic-empty` ground.
- Species without a photograph (ink only): cosmos, iris, daisy, moonflower, ume, chrysanthemum. Species without an ink drawing: camellia, hibiscus, dandelion, thistle, sunflower, magnolia. Lotus, lily, rose, peony, sakura, wisteria, hydrangea have both; the pond, petal, garland and lace images are wildcards any species may use.

## 2. Shipped files (produced by the pipeline, committed under `src/img/`)

```
src/img/covers/<id>.jpg          400×400 JPEG, quality ~0.82, colour kept; one per album (al_*), artist (ar_*), playlist (pl_*),
                                 plus liked, mosaic-empty, and one per browse category (ge_*; these are the genre tiles)
src/img/covers/<id>.thumb.jpg    only for editorial playlists: the same crop without the label strip (DESIGN 5.6 thumb rule)
src/img/deco/<name>.png          RGBA PNG, white/grey art on transparent, tightly cropped, max side 640 px
src/img/petals/<n>.png           RGBA PNG petal cut-outs, 24–40 px, at least 8 of them
src/imagery.js                   window.Imagery (below), generated; loaded before flora.js
```

`src/imagery.js` attaches exactly one global at evaluation time and touches no DOM:

```js
window.Imagery = {
  version: 1,
  base: 'img/',                     // relative to the page; build.js copies src/img → dist/img
  covers: { 'al_x': { src: 'img/covers/al_x.jpg', thumb: 'img/covers/al_x.thumb.jpg' /* only when it exists */ }, ... },
  deco: [ { id: 'branch-peony-1', src: 'img/deco/branch-peony-1.png', w: 640, h: 512, kind: 'branch',
            species: ['peony', 'ume'], stem: 'bottomleft' /* which edge or corner the art attaches to: left|right|top|bottom|topleft|topright|bottomleft|bottomright|center */,
            source: 'generated/deco_branch_home.jpg' }, ... ],
  petals: [ { src: 'img/petals/1.png', w: 32, h: 28 }, ... ],
};
```

Required decoration inventory (DESIGN 6.5 keeps its placements; each row needs at least the listed count):

| kind | needed for | species groups | count |
|---|---|---|---|
| `branch` | Home readout branch | peony/sakura/ume (the user's 7.webp blossom branch keyed from grey, probe/branch.jpg, deco_branch_home*) | ≥ 3 |
| `sprig` | hero sprig hanging from the top-right; expanded-player floor sprig rising from the bottom-left; library sprig; queue empty state | sakura, ume, peony, rose, chrysanthemum, wisteria, lily/lotus, daisy/cosmos; at least one `stem: topright` and one `stem: bottomleft` per group (a flipped copy counts) | ≥ 12 |
| `spray` | first empty slot of a short shelf | mixed | ≥ 2 |
| `bud` | Liked Songs empty state | any | ≥ 1 |
| `twig` | no results / not found | any | ≥ 1 |
| `seedling` | generic empty state | any | ≥ 1 |

Keying rule: for art on pure black, alpha = clamp((luma − 12) / 60) per pixel with the colour kept as drawn (do not premultiply twice); for 7.webp, alpha from distance to the measured grey, then despeckle. Crop every decoration to its content box plus 2 px. No decoration may contain text, a watermark, a frame line or the cherub.

Species mapping for covers: `Flora.GENRE_SPECIES` (unchanged) decides an entity's species; each species maps to a candidate list of sources (the pipeline's `imagery/assign.js` holds the table; every species has ≥ 2 candidates, mixing a photograph and an ink drawing where both exist). Assignment is deterministic: `hash(id)` picks the candidate and the crop (zoom 1.0–1.6, position, horizontal flip); two entities sharing a source must get visibly different crops. Editorial playlists keep the `> name` label strip at the bottom-left in DotGothic16, mixes keep the big outlined numeral, Top 50 keeps `50`, every playlist keeps the 13×12 pixel blossom mark at the top-right (DESIGN 5.6); render these overlays onto the cover files with the same canvas code the pipeline uses for crops. Liked Songs: the user's lotus photograph (`source/3.jpg`) cropped to the flower, with the old diagonal violet-to-mint gradient multiplied over it at low opacity so it still reads as the Liked cover. Artists: a portrait-style crop (the subject centred, since it is shown in a circle). Genre tiles (`ge_*`): a crop of that genre's species source; the name label stays an HTML element in app.js.

## 3. Runtime contract

- `Catalog.cover(entityOrId, opts)` returns `Imagery.covers[id].src` (or `.thumb` for `variant: 'thumb'` when it exists), synchronously, with no canvas. `Catalog.hasCover()` is true for every id in the manifest. In Node (no `window.Imagery`) it still returns `''` and `node src/test-data.js` still passes. `Catalog.coverSpecies()` is unchanged.
- `Catalog.playlistMosaic(trackIds)` (user playlists): composes the 2×2 mosaic from the album cover files on a canvas. Because the files load asynchronously it returns the cached data URL when ready, otherwise `''` after starting the load; when a mosaic becomes ready, data.js dispatches `document.dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent('catalog:coverready'))` once per batch, and app.js re-runs its lazy cover pump for `img[data-cover]:not([src])` on that event. Empty playlists use `img/covers/mosaic-empty.jpg` directly.
- `Flora` keeps its global, `SPECIES`, `GENRE_SPECIES`, `speciesFor`, `rng`, `hash`, `HOME_SEED`, `BLOSSOM`, `gridPath`, `sprite(opts)`, `draw(canvas, opts)`, `petals(canvas, opts)` with the same signatures. `sprite(opts)` now composes from the decoration bitmaps: pick a `deco` entry by `kind` and `species` (seeded by `opts.seed`; fall back to any entry of that kind), fit it into `w×h×dpr` with `object-fit: contain` semantics, place it by `anchor` (flip horizontally when the entry's `stem` side is opposite the anchor), optional slight scale from the seed, and return a cached PNG data URL. Until the bitmap has loaded it returns `''` and starts loading. New: `Flora.ready` (a Promise resolved when every decoration and petal bitmap has loaded or failed) and `Flora.onReady(fn)`. `Flora.petals()` draws the petal cut-outs instead of drawn shapes; with `fill: '#000'` (invert mode) it draws them as black silhouettes via `source-in`. `Flora.paintCover`, `bloom`, `leaf`, `bud`, `petal`, `branch` may remain as no-op or legacy helpers but nothing may call them for shipped visuals.
- app.js: `paintFlora` must not remove an `img[data-flora]` when `sprite()` returns `''` before `Flora.ready`; it stays pending and is painted on `Flora.onReady`. After ready, a `''` result removes it as before. Genre tiles show `Catalog.cover('ge_x')` as a filtered `<img class="cover">` under the existing black name label (the species sprite and the flat colour field go). The Home branch, library sprig, hero sprigs, dialog sprig, shelf spray and empty states keep their call sites and sizes. player.js: the expanded-player sprig repaints on `Flora.onReady` if it was empty.
- `build.js` copies `src/img/**` to `dist/img/**` (and removes stale files there). The standalone page must work when opened from disk (relative `img/` paths; no `fetch`). The publishable fragment references the same relative paths; the orchestrator publishes the files alongside it.
- Filters: covers keep `filter: var(--fx)`; decorations keep `.flora` (and `invert(1)` under invert). The monochrome default must leave no coloured pixel in mono, dither, invert or ink (the verifier's pixel scan).
- Nothing new may be fetched from the network at runtime. Total shipped image weight ≤ 6 MB.

## 4. Pipeline tooling

`imagery/pipeline/` (owner: imagery engineer) holds Node scripts that drive the headless Edge harness (`tools/cdp.js`) to do all pixel work on a canvas page served from `dist/` (copy `imagery/` to `dist/imagery/` for processing; never ship that copy): measuring content boxes, keying, cropping, overlays, JPEG/PNG export (data URL back to Node, written to `src/img/`). Font: the canvas page loads DotGothic16 from Google Fonts and waits for `document.fonts.ready` before drawing labels. `imagery/pipeline/README.md` documents how to re-run it. A contact sheet `dist/preview-imagery.html` shows every shipped cover at 48 px and 184 px, every decoration on black, and the petals, under each filter mode.
