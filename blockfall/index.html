Controls

Each action has two key slots. Click a slot, then press a key. Press Backspace to clear the slot.

Handling

DCD (DAS cut delay) pauses auto-shift for the new piece after a lock. SDF is the soft drop speed as a multiple of gravity, and 41 is instant. IRS / IHS rotates or holds the new piece at once if you hold a rotate or hold key when it appears.

Opponent

In Versus the first player to top out loses the round. Your attack first cancels your own incoming garbage, and the rest goes to the other well. The bar left of each well shows incoming lines. Red lines rise when you lock a piece that clears nothing. Yellow lines are still on the way. Difficulty sets how well and how fast the bot plays. A change starts with its next piece. Same pieces gives you both the same pieces each round. Garbage messiness is the chance that the hole moves between the lines of one attack.
